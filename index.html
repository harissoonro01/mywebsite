About Us

Ittifaq Feeds (Pvt.) Ltd. is a trusted producer of premium poultry feed, located in the Port Qasim Industrial Zone, Karachi. With a focus on quality, innovation, and consistency, we provide scientifically formulated feed solutions to support the growth and health of poultry across Pakistan and neighboring regions. Our modern manufacturing facility is equipped with advanced technology and managed by skilled professionals, ensuring high standards of nutrition, hygiene, and production efficiency. We are committed to helping poultry farmers succeed by delivering reliable, performance-driven feed backed by expert support and long-term partnership.
